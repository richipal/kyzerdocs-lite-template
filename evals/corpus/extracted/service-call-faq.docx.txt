Cascade HVAC Services - Customer Service FAQ

Document ID: CSC-FAQ-v3. Service area: 25-mile radius of the Springfield depot.

1. What does a diagnostic visit cost?

The standard diagnostic fee is $89. The fee is waived if the customer authorizes the recommended repair during the same visit.

2. What about after-hours emergencies?

Emergency after-hours service carries a $175 dispatch fee, with a target response window of 90 minutes within the standard service area.

3. How is labor billed?

The labor rate is $145 per hour, billed in 15-minute increments after the first hour.

4. What is the parts and labor warranty?

OEM parts carry a 1-year warranty. Labor on repairs carries a 90-day warranty.

5. Is financing available?

The Cascade Comfort Card offers 0% APR for 12 months on system installs over $2,500.

6. How often should filters be replaced?

We recommend replacing 1-inch filters every 60 to 90 days, and 4-inch media filters every 6 months.

7. What does the annual maintenance plan include?

The annual maintenance plan costs $189 per year and includes two tune-up visits: one spring AC tune-up and one fall furnace tune-up.

8. Which refrigerant do new installs use?

New installs since 2025 use R-454B refrigerant. R-410A systems are being phased out.

9. Who pulls permits?

Cascade pulls city permits for full system replacements. The customer is responsible for permits on DIY-adjacent repairs not performed by Cascade.

10. What is the cancellation policy for scheduled appointments?

There is no fee for cancellations made 4 or more hours in advance. A $50 fee applies to no-shows.
