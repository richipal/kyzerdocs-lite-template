Sunrise Dental Studio - Appointment Cancellation and No-Show Policy
Policy Number: SDS-CANCEL-09
Effective Date: September 1, 2025

1. Notice Required
Patients must cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours before their scheduled appointment time by calling (555) 019-2200 or by using the online patient portal.

2. No-Show and Late-Cancellation Fees
- First occurrence in a rolling 12-month window: $45 fee.
- Second occurrence in the same window: $95 fee.
- Third occurrence: the patient is moved to same-day-confirmation-only scheduling for 6 months.

3. Same-Day Appointment Exception
Appointments booked within 24 hours of the visit are exempt from the cancellation-notice requirement, but a $25 fee still applies if the patient does not call ahead to cancel.

4. Hygiene Appointments
Routine hygiene (cleaning) appointments run 50 minutes. New-patient hygiene appointments run 75 minutes to include full-mouth X-rays and a periodontal charting.

5. Late Arrivals
Patients arriving more than 15 minutes late to a 30-minute or shorter appointment will be asked to reschedule. Patients arriving more than 20 minutes late to appointments longer than 30 minutes will be asked to reschedule.

6. Insurance Pre-Authorization
For procedures over $500, Sunrise Dental Studio submits a pre-authorization request to the patient's insurance carrier. Typical carrier turnaround is 10 to 15 business days; treatment is not scheduled until pre-authorization is returned unless the patient elects to self-pay.

7. Fee Waivers
Cancellation fees are waived for documented medical emergencies (doctor's note required within 5 business days) and for active-duty military deployment orders.

8. Recall Reminders
The office sends recall reminders at 3 intervals: 30 days, 14 days, and 3 days before a scheduled cleaning, via text, email, or phone call depending on the patient's stated preference on Form SDS-PREF-01.
