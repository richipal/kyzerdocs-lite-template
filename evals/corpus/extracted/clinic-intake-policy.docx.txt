Riverside Family Clinic - Patient Intake and Administrative Policy

Policy Number: RFC-INTAKE-014

Effective Date: March 3, 2025. Revised: January 12, 2026.

1. Appointment Lengths

New patient intake appointments are scheduled for 45 minutes. Established patient follow-up appointments are scheduled for 20 minutes. Walk-in urgent slots are limited to 3 per day, first-come-first-served, starting at 8:00 AM.

2. Cancellation and No-Show Fees

Patients must cancel at least 24 hours in advance by calling 555-0142 or using the patient portal. The fee for a first missed appointment without 24-hour notice is $35. The fee for a second missed appointment within a rolling 12-month period is $75.

3. Required Intake Documents

New patients must complete Form RFC-14A (Medical History), Form RFC-14B (Insurance Authorization), and present a government-issued photo ID at the first visit. Minors under 18 additionally require Form RFC-14C (Parental Consent) signed by a legal guardian.

4. HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices

Every new patient must sign Form RFC-HIPAA-01 (Notice of Privacy Practices acknowledgment) before the first clinical encounter. Signed forms are retained for 6 years per clinic record-retention policy.

5. Insurance Verification

Front-desk staff complete insurance verification within 2 business days of an appointment being scheduled. Patients are notified by phone if verification cannot be completed before the visit.

6. Sliding-Scale Fee Program

Patients with household income at or below 200% of the federal poverty line may apply for the sliding-scale fee program. Applications are processed within 5 business days of submission.

7. Clinic Hours

The clinic is open Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:30 PM, and is closed for lunch from 12:30 PM to 1:15 PM daily.

8. Medical Records Requests

Patients requesting a copy of their medical records must submit Form RFC-RR-02. Standard turnaround time for records requests is 10 business days from the date the form is received.
