Ironclad Electrical Contractors - PPE and Lockout/Tagout Policy

Document ID: IEC-PPE-POLICY-06. Applies to all field technicians and apprentices.

1. Arc-Flash PPE Categories

Arc-flash PPE Category 2 is required for incident energy above 1.2 cal/cm2. Arc-flash PPE Category 4 is required for incident energy above 8 cal/cm2, up to 40 cal/cm2.

2. Lockout/Tagout Voltage Threshold

Lockout/tagout procedures are mandatory on any circuit rated 50 volts or greater before work begins.

3. Panel Lug Torque Specifications

Per manufacturer torque chart IEC-014, panel lugs are torqued to 35 in-lb for 14 to 10 AWG conductors, and 50 in-lb for 8 to 6 AWG conductors.

4. PPE Inspection Schedule

Technicians perform a daily visual inspection of PPE before use. A documented inspection is completed monthly on Form IEC-PPE-07.

5. Insulated Gloves

Insulated gloves are Class 00, rated to 500V AC. Gloves are tested every 6 months and replaced immediately if any puncture is found.

6. Hard Hat Replacement

Hard hats are replaced every 5 years from the date of manufacture, or immediately after any impact.

7. Lockout/Tagout Devices

Each crew truck carries a minimum of 2 lockout locks. Tags must include the technician ID and the date the lock was applied.

8. Weekly Safety Meeting

A mandatory toolbox talk is held every Monday at 7:00 AM and documented on Form IEC-TB-02.
