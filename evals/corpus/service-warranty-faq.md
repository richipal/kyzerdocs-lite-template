# BrightPath Landscaping - Service and Warranty FAQ

Document ID: BPL-FAQ-2026-02

## How is mowing and maintenance priced?

Standard weekly mowing is billed at $0.008 per square foot of managed lawn area, with a $65 minimum visit charge. Biweekly service carries a 15% surcharge over the weekly per-square-foot rate.

## What is covered under the planting warranty?

Trees and shrubs installed by BrightPath carry a 1-year replacement warranty covering plant death from transplant shock or defective stock. Annuals and seasonal color are not covered. Sod carries a 30-day establishment warranty, contingent on the customer following the posted watering schedule (Form BPL-SOD-03).

## What are the seasonal service windows?

- Spring cleanup: March 1 through April 15.
- Mulch installation: April 15 through May 31, at $95 per cubic yard installed.
- Fall cleanup and leaf removal: October 15 through December 1.
- Irrigation winterization: October 1 through November 15, flat fee of $85 per zone up to 6 zones.

## How does the irrigation system inspection work?

Spring irrigation startup and inspection is $120 for systems with up to 8 zones, plus $12 per additional zone. Leaks identified during startup are quoted separately and are not included in the startup fee.

## What is the cancellation policy for a season-long contract?

Season-long maintenance contracts require 30 days written notice to cancel without penalty. Cancelling with less than 30 days notice incurs a fee equal to 2 weeks of the standard service rate.

## Does BrightPath handle pesticide and fertilizer applications?

Yes. The 6-application seasonal fertilization and weed-control program is billed at $340 per season for lawns up to 8,000 square feet, with a $0.04 per square foot surcharge above that size. All applicators hold a current state Category 3B pesticide license.
